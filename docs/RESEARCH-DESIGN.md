# Rrënja Dental — what real dental practices' sites do (research, 2026-10-12)

Done after the owner rejected the first three pictures: they showed a wooden table in a room and said
nothing of a dentist. The mistake was in the brief (it asked for "a calm interior") and in sending the
pictures on without judging them. This file is what the design is now drawn from.

Marked OBSERVED (the page was opened and read) or REPORTED (taken from a review of sites that I did not
open myself).

## Sites

- OBSERVED, harleystreetdentalstudio.com: headline is a claim with a date ("Award-winning Harley Street
  dentist since 2004"); "Book online" in the hero and in the header, plus WhatsApp and the phone; awards as
  cards; patient stories on video; a Google rating with the number of reviews and six excerpts; four
  treatment cards with pictures; a quote from a named senior dentist with his registration; a "smile
  gallery" and a "prices" menu; "Relax, this is no ordinary dental clinic" (nervous patients are spoken to
  directly); the languages spoken.
- OBSERVED, banningdental.co.uk: a price on the first screen ("Dental checkup only £30 including X-Rays"),
  "No misleading 'from' prices, no hidden extras", a free consultation button, a virtual consultation, a
  price-match promise. No dentists' profiles on the home page.
- hellotend.com answered 403 to an automated reader: not seen.
- REPORTED (Studio 8E8, "The Best Dental Practice Websites of 2025"; Azuro Digital, "10 Best Dental
  Website Designs of 2026"): Arbor (dark, calm palette, refined type, subtle hover motion); Boulder Smiles
  (natural light, lifestyle photographs); Center for Advanced Dentistry (generous before-and-after
  pictures); Dentologie (bold type, "Book now" everywhere, transparent prices); Grand Street Dental
  (magazine look, photographs of the designed office, before-and-after galleries); Zen Dental Studio (calm
  palette, white space, costs and plans explained, online booking); The Tooth Co. (authentic team pictures,
  before-and-after galleries); Staten Island Oral Surgery (a rating badge and affiliations on the first
  screen).

## What they have in common, and what Rrënja Dental must therefore have

1. The first screen says "dentist" by its picture: a treatment room with the chair and the lamp, a dentist
   at work, or a smile. Never a neutral interior.
2. Booking is one press away on every screen, with the phone beside it.
3. Proof before promises: a rating with the number of reviews, named dentists with their qualifications,
   before-and-after pictures, real words of patients.
4. Prices said plainly, or at least "from", and what a first visit costs.
5. Fear is addressed in words: painless, calm, explained before it is done.
6. Each dentist is a person: portrait, speciality, what they do, their own rating, their own booking link.
7. Practical facts without searching: hours, address with a map, emergency number, languages.
8. Motion with restraint: a hero that settles, pictures that reveal; nothing that delays booking.

## Open-source dental software looked at for the dentist's tools

- OBSERVED, clawnify/open-dentist (MIT, small and new): an agenda by treatment room with 15-minute slots
  and a "now" line; a patient page with tabs: overview with medical-alert badges, a tooth chart (FDI
  numbering, a brush per condition: caries, restoration, crown, root treatment, implant, missing), a
  treatment plan with a fee and a status per item (planned, accepted, completed, declined) and totals, a
  timeline of clinical notes by practitioner, billing with billed, paid and balance; treatment types with
  a default duration, fee and colour; a waiting list in its schema.
- OBSERVED (topic page), ZoliQua/React-Advanced-Odontogram (78 stars): an interactive tooth chart in React
  with periodontal charting and three numbering systems: a candidate for the tooth chart instead of drawing
  one ourselves, licence to be read first.
- OBSERVED (topic page), GDguzi/GD-DentOS: besides records and appointments, inventory and sterilisation
  tracking; thefinalcutbg/QDento: a complete free desktop practice program.
- None of the projects seen stores X-ray pictures with the visit; that part is ours to design.

## The three directions drawn from this (design/directions)

All three show a dental treatment room or a dentist at work, the six services with a starting price, three
dentists with portraits and ratings, before-and-after pictures, patients' reviews and the booking band with
the deposit. A: warm and established (cream, ink blue, terracotta, serif headlines). B: clean and clinical
(white, navy, sky blue, rounded). C: premium and dark (charcoal green, ivory, gold-sand).

The texts and the people in the pictures were generated and are placeholders: no price, award, standard or
licence number in them is real, and the portraits are of nobody.
